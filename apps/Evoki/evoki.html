Our design philosophy
Calm.
Dignified.
Never
overwhelming.
Principle 01
One thing at a time
The screen never competes with itself. Every moment has one clear action — and the rest of the app politely steps back.
Principle 02
No streaks. No shame.
Evoki never punishes missed days. There are no fire emojis, no broken records. It simply shows up again tomorrow.
Principle 03
Senior-first, always
Clear, large text. High contrast. Generous tap areas. Because everything in Evoki is designed to feel effortless — no squinting, no fumbling.
