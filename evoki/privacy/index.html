Who we are

Evoki is made by Titil Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17308546). Our registered office is 71-75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London WC2H 9JQ, United Kingdom.

Titil Ltd is the data controller for your information. This means we decide how and why it is used, and we are responsible for looking after it.

What we collect

  • Account: your first name, your email address (from Google or Apple sign-in), whether you are a senior or a family member, your language and display settings, and your age if you choose to give it.
  • Daily use: check-in answers (sleep, mood, energy and time with others), daily plans and finished activities, brain game results, journal entries, memories, recall notes and saved articles.
  • Medicines: medicine names, colours, times, reminders and whether doses were taken.
  • Care Circle: your Care Code, the family members you connect, their nicknames, and the messages they send you.
  • Help contact: the name and phone number of the person you add as your help contact.
  • Apple Health, only if you turn it on: steps, sleep and heart rate. Evoki only reads this; it never writes to Apple Health.
  • Device: a device identifier and a notification token, so we can send notifications and show the devices you are signed in on.

Family members give us their first name, email address and the messages and medicine reminders they send.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

We use your information only to run Evoki for you: to plan your day, show your progress, send reminders and messages, and let the family members you choose support you.

  • To provide the app you asked for, we rely on our contract with you (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Check-ins, medicines and Apple Health data are health-related, which the law treats as special category data. For these we rely on your explicit consent, which you give in the app before Evoki keeps them (UK GDPR Article 9(2)(a)).

You can withdraw your consent at any time. See Your rights. Withdrawing consent does not affect what we did before you withdrew it.

What connected family can see

A senior chooses who to connect by sharing their Care Code. Family members connected this way can see:

  • the senior's name, check-in answers and recent history;
  • today's plan and which activities are done;
  • the senior's medicines and whether doses were taken;
  • steps, sleep and heart rate, if the senior has turned Apple Health on;
  • the date of the senior's last journal entry, but not what they wrote.

Journal text, memories and private notes are not shared. A senior can remove any family member at any time in Care Circle, and a family member can leave at any time.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • We do not use your information to train AI models. Short texts, such as a weekly summary, are written by Apple Intelligence on your own phone and do not leave it.
  • We do not collect your contacts, your location or your photos. The camera is only used to scan a Care Code, and voice input is turned into text on your phone.

How messages are protected

Messages from family to a senior are encrypted on the sender's phone with the senior's own key before they reach our servers. The key to read them stays on the senior's devices, so we cannot read the text of these messages. Notification previews only say that a new message has arrived.

Where your information is stored

On your phone, your information is protected by iOS file encryption. In the cloud, it is stored in Google Firebase, in London (Google Cloud region europe-west2).

Sign-in and notifications use Google and Apple services that may process information outside the UK, including in the United States. Two small server functions currently run in the United States (Google Cloud region us-central1).

Companies that help us run Evoki

These companies process information for us, under contract, only to provide their service:

  • Google (Firebase): database, sign-in, server functions, notifications and app security checks.
  • Google Sign-In, if you sign in with Google.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: when the app crashes or a sync fails, it sends a technical report: where in the app it happened, an error code, your device model, iOS and app version, and a random installation ID. It never includes your name, messages, health information or medicines. We use these reports to find and fix problems. [FOUNDER TO CONFIRM: lawful basis for crash reports, e.g. legitimate interests]
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, notifications, and Apple Health on your phone.

Subscriptions and usage statistics are not switched on at the moment. If we turn them on (RevenueCat for subscriptions, PostHog for usage statistics), we will update this policy first.

How long we keep it

  • Your account information: until you delete your account.
  • Messages: deleted automatically 90 days after they are sent.
  • When you delete your account: an automatic process removes your information from our database, and Evoki removes it from your phone. A family member's link to you is kept only as a record that the connection has ended, without your information.

Your rights

You can:

  • see and change your information in the app;
  • turn Apple Health off in Settings;
  • remove family members in Care Circle;
  • withdraw your consent and delete everything by deleting your account in Settings;
  • ask us for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct it, delete it or stop using it, by emailing us. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, please tell us first so we can try to put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Children

Evoki is intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how your health information is used, Evoki will ask for your agreement again before you continue.
